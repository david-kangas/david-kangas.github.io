Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 19, 2026

Coach Method is an iPhone application developed by Kangas Works.

We believe useful software should respect your privacy. Coach Method is designed to operate entirely on your device.

Information Collection

Coach Method does not collect, transmit, or store personal information on external servers.

The application does not require an account or user registration.

Information Stored on Your Device

Coach Method may store application preferences, such as your selected chart color, locally on your iPhone.

This information is used only to support the application's functionality and is not transmitted to Kangas Works.

Analytics and Advertising

Coach Method does not include third-party analytics, advertising services, or behavioral tracking.

Third-Party Services

Coach Method does not require external services to perform its core functions.

The application may provide links to external websites or App Store listings. Those services operate under their own privacy policies.

Children's Privacy

Coach Method does not knowingly collect personal information from children or adults.

Changes to This Policy

If Coach Method's privacy practices change, this policy will be updated to reflect those changes.
