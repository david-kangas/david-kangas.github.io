AI Governance & Risk Management

AI Governance That Doesn't Slow Your Team Down

I help organizations identify friction in AI workflows and build systems that manage risk without disrupting how teams operate.

Areas of Focus

  • Identify friction and risk across AI-enabled workflows.
  • Design governance frameworks aligned with how teams actually operate.
  • Translate evolving AI regulations into practical, operational controls.

Approach

Most AI governance introduces friction because it is layered on after the fact, through policies that don't match how teams actually work.

I focus on identifying where friction already exists in AI workflows, then designing systems that integrate directly into operations.

The goal isn't just compliance. It's enabling teams to move quickly with the right controls in place.

Example Work

Designed a structured intake and decision system to standardize client evaluation and reduce ambiguity in operational workflows.

Result: Improved consistency in decision-making and reduced subjective variance across cases.
