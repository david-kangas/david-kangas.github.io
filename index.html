About Kangas Works

Kangas Works is an independent software studio founded by David Kangas.

Our approach combines practical problem-solving with thoughtful software design. We look for unnecessary complexity, identify what matters, and build tools that do their jobs without getting in the user's way.

Good software doesn't need to do everything. It needs to do the right things well.
